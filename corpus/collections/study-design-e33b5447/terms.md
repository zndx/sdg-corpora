# Grounding terms

The ontology universals this collection's chapters are written around (each an sdg: class, grounded in a BFO/CCO genus).

## Study Design
- **genus**: `bfo:0000015`
- **definition**: A conceptual plan and methodological framework for conducting nutritional anthropology research, specifying questions, data needs, and methods.
- **attributes**: designId, designName, designType, startDate, endDate, status, primaryObjective
- **relations**: usesMethod→ResearchMethod, assessesPopulation→Population, generatesData→Dataset

## Research Method
- **genus**: `bfo:0000015`
- **definition**: A standardized procedure or technique used to collect, analyze, or interpret data in nutritional anthropology, such as anthropometry or dietary analysis.
- **attributes**: methodId, methodName, methodCategory, accuracyLevel, biasRisk, isStandardized
- **relations**: appliedTo→StudyDesign, measuresAttribute→BiologicalMeasurement, usedIn→Dataset

## Biological Measurement
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A quantified physical or physiological attribute of an individual or population, such as body mass, height, or energy expenditure, recorded during a study.
- **attributes**: measurementId, measurementValue, unitOfMeasure, measurementDate, measurementType, referenceStandard, status
- **relations**: recordedIn→Dataset, measuredBy→ResearchMethod, pertainsTo→Individual

## Individual
- **genus**: `bfo:0000002`
- **definition**: A single human participant enrolled in a nutritional anthropology study, characterized by demographic and biological attributes.
- **attributes**: participantId, age, sex, ethnicity, enrollmentDate, consentStatus
- **relations**: participatesIn→StudyDesign, providesMeasurement→BiologicalMeasurement, belongsToList→Population

## Population
- **genus**: `bfo:0000040`
- **definition**: A defined group of individuals sharing common characteristics, used for surveillance, monitoring, or comparative analysis in nutritional research.
- **attributes**: populationId, populationName, geographicRegion, sampleSize, inclusionCriteria, monitoringStatus
- **relations**: includesIndividuals→Individual, studiedBy→StudyDesign, hasReferenceData→ReferenceDataset

## Dataset
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000958`
- **definition**: A structured collection of recorded data, including measurements, survey responses, or observational notes, generated by a research method.
- **attributes**: datasetId, datasetName, format, creationDate, recordCount, dataQualityScore, storageLocation
- **relations**: generatedBy→ResearchMethod, containsMeasurements→BiologicalMeasurement, linkedToStudy→StudyDesign

## Ethical Protocol
- **genus**: `bfo:0000015`
- **definition**: A formalized set of principles and procedures governing the conduct of research, including informed consent, recruitment, and data handling.
- **attributes**: protocolId, protocolName, approvalDate, reviewingBody, principles, status
- **relations**: governs→StudyDesign, requiresConsentFrom→Individual, appliesTo→Dataset
