# Directive Governance in GDPR Compliance Auditing

This chapter presents five relational tables that instantiate the axioms attestation_with_supporting_evidence, gdpr_lawful_basis, verification_produces_evidence, gdpr_article_subclass, and control_with_evidence_requirement. The tables encode directive instances, evidence requirements, and lawful-basis linkages drawn from GDPR compliance records. Primary-key and foreign-key columns enable deterministic cross-table navigation that recovers multi-hop facts such as the evidence produced for a given attestation under a stated article.

att-010 → lb-101

Next resolve the control referenced by lb-101:

lb-101 → ctr-001

- art-030 references ctr-001
- ver-020 references lb-101

attestation=att-010, lawful_basis=lb-101, control=ctr-001, article=art-030, verification=ver-020.
