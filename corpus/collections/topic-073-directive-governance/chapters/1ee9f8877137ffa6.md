# Chapter 4: GDPR Attestations and Directive Governance in School Oversight

This chapter presents a relational model for recording GDPR attestations, lawful bases, and associated audit activities within educational governance. The model uses five tables derived directly from the ontology axioms to support traceability across processes, persons, and requirements. Data instances reflect activities at Heaton St. Barnabas CE Primary School during the 2016-17 monitoring period.

## 2 Cross-Join Demonstration

To determine the person who signed the attestation whose supporting evidence rests on the lawful basis that satisfies requirement req-001, join t_attestation_with_supporting_evidence to t_gdpr_lawful_basis on y = id, then to t_requirement_equiv_specifies_target on the latter’s y column, and finally to t_attestation_signed_by on matching attestation id. The joined row for att-010 yields: attestation id att-010, lawful basis lb-001, requirement req-001, signed by John Wedgwood Pound.
