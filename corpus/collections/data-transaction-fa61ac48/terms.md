# Grounding terms

The ontology universals this collection's chapters are written around (each an sdg: class, grounded in a BFO/CCO genus).

## Data Transaction
- **genus**: `bfo:0000015`
- **definition**: An automated business process activity involving the secure exchange or processing of confidential data between systems, suppliers, or customers.
- **attributes**: transactionId, timestamp, protocol, dataVolumeBytes, status, complianceStandard
- **relations**: usesEncryptionKey→EncryptionKey, involvesDataFile→DataFile, executedByScript→AutomatedScript, generatesAuditTrail→AuditTrail

## Data File
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A digital record or sample of business-critical information, such as a file or email, that is stored on a server or transmitted between systems.
- **attributes**: fileId, fileName, fileSizeBytes, storageLocation, isEncrypted, isDeletedSecurely, compressionRatio
- **relations**: storedOnServer→Server, partOfTransaction→DataTransaction, hasDigitalSignature→DigitalSignature

## Encryption Key
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A cryptographic standard or parameter used by the PGP Encryption Platform to encrypt and decrypt data in transit and at rest.
- **attributes**: keyId, keyType, creationDate, expirationDate, isRevoked, managedByServer
- **relations**: managedByServer→Server, usedInTransaction→DataTransaction

## Automated Script
- **genus**: `bfo:0000015`
- **definition**: A script-driven business process or program that integrates with the PGP Command Line to automatically encrypt, decrypt, or transfer data.
- **attributes**: scriptId, scriptName, platform, isLegacy, executionFrequency
- **relations**: runsOnServer→Server, initiatesTransaction→DataTransaction

## Server
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A physical or virtual computing system that stores data files, manages encryption keys, or executes automated scripts within the enterprise infrastructure.
- **attributes**: serverId, serverName, operatingSystem, isBreached, location
- **relations**: hostsDataFile→DataFile, managesEncryptionKey→EncryptionKey, runsAutomatedScript→AutomatedScript

## Audit Trail
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A digital record or dataset generated by digital signatures to provide long-term verification of the authenticity of the sender and content of a data transaction.
- **attributes**: trailId, generatedTimestamp, senderIdentity, contentHash, isVerified
- **relations**: linkedToTransaction→DataTransaction, createdBySignature→DigitalSignature

## Digital Signature
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A cryptographic mechanism applied to a data file to ensure its authenticity and integrity, enabling the recipient to verify the sender and content.
- **attributes**: signatureId, algorithm, signingDate, isValid
- **relations**: signsDataFile→DataFile, usesEncryptionKey→EncryptionKey, generatesAuditTrail→AuditTrail
