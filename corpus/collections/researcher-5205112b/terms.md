# Grounding terms

The ontology universals this collection's chapters are written around (each an sdg: class, grounded in a BFO/CCO genus).

## Young Researcher
- **genus**: `bfo:0000040`
- **definition**: A person holding a PhD degree awarded within six years prior to participation in EYE activities, engaged in high-risk FET research.
- **attributes**: researcherId, full_name, phd_award_date, current_affiliation, email_address, is_selected_for_funding
- **relations**: participatedIn→Event, belongs_to→ResearchTeam, submitted_idea_to→ResearchIdea

## EYE Activity Event
- **genus**: `bfo:0000015`
- **definition**: A scheduled regional or European brainstorming, training, or networking activity such as a Lab Surfing workshop, Blue Sky conference, or Science Incubator summer school.
- **attributes**: event_id, event_name, event_type, start_date, end_date, location_region, is_funded
- **relations**: hosted_by→PartnerOrganization, attracts_participants→Researcher, focuses_on_themes→ResearchTheme

## EYE Partner Organization
- **genus**: `bfo:0000040`
- **definition**: An institution, university, research center, or SME that has joined the EYE project consortium to coordinate or execute activities.
- **attributes**: org_id, org_name, org_type, country_code, is_coordinator, is_originator
- **relations**: coordinates_project→PartnerOrganization, hosts_event→Event, establishes_local_network_in→Country

## Scientific Research Idea
- **genus**: `bfo:0000040`
- **definition**: A high-risk, interdisciplinary scientific concept or proposal generated by young researchers during brainstorming sessions, intended for future FET project proposals.
- **attributes**: idea_id, idea_title, risk_level, is_selected_for_incubator, is_selected_for_funding
- **relations**: generated_by_team→ResearchTeam, aligns_with_theme→ResearchTheme, targets_societal_challenge→SocietalChallenge

## Collaborative Research Team
- **genus**: `bfo:0000040`
- **definition**: A group of young researchers collaborating on a specific high-risk scientific idea, formed through networking and brainstorming events.
- **attributes**: team_id, team_name, formation_region, is_formed_for_incubator
- **relations**: consists_of_members→Researcher, develops_idea→ResearchIdea, attends_event→Event

## FET Research Theme
- **genus**: `bfo:0000040`
- **definition**: A broad multidisciplinary research area or convergence topic that provides the thematic framework for EYE activities and FET-quality research.
- **attributes**: theme_id, theme_name, is_disruptive, is_interdisciplinary
- **relations**: informs_event→Event, guides_idea→ResearchIdea

## Horizon 2020 Societal Challenge
- **genus**: `bfo:0000040`
- **definition**: A broad societal goal or policy area from the Horizon 2020 framework that EYE activities build thematically upon.
- **attributes**: challenge_id, challenge_name, is_policy_relevant
- **relations**: targeted_by_idea→ResearchIdea

## European Country
- **genus**: `bfo:0000040`
- **definition**: A sovereign state in Europe where EYE partners are located or where local collaborator networks are established.
- **attributes**: country_code, country_name, has_partner, has_local_network
- **relations**: hosts_partner→PartnerOrganization, has_local_network_established_by→PartnerOrganization
