# Grounding terms

The ontology universals this collection's chapters are written around (each an sdg: class, grounded in a BFO/CCO genus).

## Controlling Report
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A structured business report generated by the FACT system for executive review, planning, or strategic monitoring.
- **attributes**: reportId, reportName, reportType, creationDate, status, layoutFormat
- **relations**: generatedBy→BusinessProcess, coversSegment→BusinessSegment, createdBy→ControllingUser

## Business Segment
- **genus**: `bfo:0000004`
- **definition**: A distinct operational division or subsidiary of the airport enterprise, such as retail, properties, or flight operations.
- **attributes**: segmentCode, segmentName, segmentType, isConsolidated, parentOrganizationId
- **relations**: generatesReport→ControllingReport, partOf→BusinessSegment

## Controlling User
- **genus**: `bfo:0000002`
- **definition**: A personnel entity within the corporate controlling department who interacts with the BI system to perform planning, reporting, or analysis tasks.
- **attributes**: userId, userName, roleTitle, department, isStrategicPlanner
- **relations**: createsReport→ControllingReport, monitorsStrategy→StrategicPlan

## Strategic Plan
- **genus**: `bfo:0000040`
- **definition**: A driver-based business plan created, executed, and monitored through the integrated BI environment to guide corporate strategy.
- **attributes**: planId, planName, planningHorizon, isDriverBased, approvalStatus, lastReviewDate
- **relations**: monitoredBy→ControllingUser, drivesSegment→BusinessSegment, producesReport→ControllingReport

## Business Process
- **genus**: `bfo:0000015`
- **definition**: A standardized operational activity supported by the BI tool, such as group consolidation, accounting, or profit and loss statement creation.
- **attributes**: processId, processName, processCategory, isAutomated, executionFrequency
- **relations**: generatesReport→ControllingReport, supportsSegment→BusinessSegment, executedBy→ControllingUser
