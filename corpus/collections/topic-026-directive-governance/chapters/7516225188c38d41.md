# Chapter 4. Directive Governance in Audit Trail Systems

This chapter presents five relational tables that instantiate axioms for directive governance, lawful basis, jurisdiction, evidence requirements, and attestation processes. The model supports cross-table queries that trace compliance chains from policies through controls to signed attestations.

## 1 Scope and Table Construction

The tables embody five Manchester OWL axioms drawn from the cco:DirectiveICE and bfo:Process hierarchies. Each table is introduced by a one-sentence statement of the axiom it realizes, followed by its primary-key and foreign-key structure and one example mental cross-join.

### 1.1 t_gdpr_lawful_basis

This table realizes AXIOM 1 by recording every directive class that possesses a lawful basis. Column id is the primary key; column y is a foreign key referencing t_policy_with_jurisdiction.id, enabling a join that retrieves the jurisdiction governing each lawful basis.

| lb-002 | Contract | pol-102 |
| lb-003 | LegalObligation | pol-103 |

### 1.2 t_policy_with_jurisdiction

This table realizes AXIOM 2 by associating each directive with its jurisdictional scope. Column id is the primary key; column y is a foreign key referencing t_gdpr_lawful_basis.id, permitting a join that recovers the lawful basis for any given jurisdiction.

### 1.3 t_attestation_with_supporting_evidence

### 1.4 t_control_with_evidence_requirement

## 2 Cross-Join Demonstration

To determine the jurisdiction and signatory for the lawful basis of the attestation “DataProcessingAudit”, join t_attestation_with_supporting_evidence to t_gdpr_lawful_basis on y = id, then to t_policy_with_jurisdiction on y = id, and finally to t_attestation_signed_by on id. The resulting row is:

att-201 | DataProcessingAudit | lb-001 | Consent | pol-101 | EU-GDPR | Dr. Elena Voss
