# Grounding terms

The ontology universals this collection's chapters are written around (each an sdg: class, grounded in a BFO/CCO genus).

## Regulatory Violation
- **genus**: `bfo:0000015`
- **definition**: A documented instance of non-compliance with a specific regulatory requirement identified during an inspection.
- **attributes**: violationId, description, regulatoryReference, severityLevel, status, inspectionReportId, dateIdentified
- **relations**: reportedIn→InspectionReport, addressedBy→CorrectiveActionPlan

## Inspection Report
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000958`
- **definition**: An official document generated by a regulatory body detailing findings from a site inspection.
- **attributes**: reportNumber, issueDate, issuingAgency, docketNumber, classification, adamsAccessionNumber
- **relations**: citesViolation→RegulatoryViolation, issuedTo→Licensee

## Corrective Action Plan
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A formal plan detailing steps taken or to be taken to resolve a regulatory violation and ensure future compliance.
- **attributes**: planId, submissionDate, complianceDeadline, status, correctiveStepsTaken, preventiveMeasures
- **relations**: addressesViolation→RegulatoryViolation, submittedBy→Licensee

## Licensee
- **genus**: `bfo:0000040`
- **definition**: An individual or organization holding a license to possess or use regulated materials.
- **attributes**: licenseNumber, licenseeName, licenseType, docketNumber, address, radiationSafetyOfficer
- **relations**: holdsLicense→RegulatoryViolation, submitsResponse→CorrectiveActionPlan

## Radiation Survey
- **genus**: `bfo:0000015`
- **definition**: A measurement process conducted to assess radiation levels in controlled or unrestricted areas.
- **attributes**: surveyId, surveyDate, areaType, measuredDoseRate, unitOfMeasure, complianceStatus
- **relations**: performedBy→Licensee, supportsCompliance→RegulatoryViolation
