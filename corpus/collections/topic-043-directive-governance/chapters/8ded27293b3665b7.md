# Chapter 7: Directive Verification and Attestation in Governance Records

This chapter presents five relational tables that instantiate axioms for directive information content entities, processes of audit and attestation, and their required linkages to persons and periods. The tables support cross-table queries that resolve verification, conduct, and signature constraints on corporate and ministerial records.

## 1 Table Structures and Axiom Embodiment

### 1.1 Requirement Verification Linkage

The table t_requirement_min_one_verification embodies axiom 1 by recording each directive information content entity X together with at least one verification entity Y. Column id is the primary key. Columns x and y function as entity references; any query joining on y to another table’s x column traverses the mandatory verification relation.

| id | x | y |
|----|---|---|
| req-01 | Court Convened Meeting 15 Dec 2015 | Attendance Slip 15 Dec 2015 |
| req-02 | Ministerial Diary Feb 2016 | Diary Entry 01 Feb 2016 |
| req-03 | AUTUMN HOLIDAY PARTY | BOD Minutes 07 Oct 2003 |
| req-04 | ESTROGEN ZONE Session | Marketing Committee Report |
| req-05 | TRIPE Event 5 Crosby | UN Tribute Log 2003 |
| req-06 | Cabinet Meeting 01 Feb 2016 | Community Cabinet Record |

### 1.2 Audit Conduct Record

The table t_audit_conducted_by embodies axiom 2 by associating each audit process X with a conducting person. Column id is the primary key. Column x references the audited process; linkage to t_audit_for_period on x yields the period of any conducted audit.

| id | x |
|----|---|
| aud-01 | Court Convened Meeting 15 Dec 2015 |
| aud-02 | Ministerial Diary Feb 2016 |
| aud-03 | AUTUMN HOLIDAY PARTY |
| aud-04 | ESTROGEN ZONE Session |
| aud-05 | TRIPE Event 5 Crosby |
| aud-06 | Cabinet Meeting 01 Feb 2016 |

### 1.3 Attestation Minimum Signer Constraint

The table t_attestation_min_one_signer embodies axiom 3 by ensuring each attestation process X carries at least one signer reference. Column id is the primary key. Column x references the attested process; cross-reference with t_attestation_signed_by on x confirms the signer cardinality.

| id | x |
|----|---|
| att-01 | Court Convened Meeting 15 Dec 2015 |
| att-02 | Ministerial Diary Feb 2016 |
| att-03 | AUTUMN HOLIDAY PARTY |

### 1.4 Attestation Signer Detail

The table t_attestation_signed_by embodies axiom 4 by recording each attestation process X and its signer person. Column id is the primary key. Column x references the attested process; join on x to t_attestation_min_one_signer validates the minimum-signer rule.

| id | x |
|----|---|
| sig-01 | Court Convened Meeting 15 Dec 2015 |
| sig-02 | Ministerial Diary Feb 2016 |
| sig-03 | AUTUMN HOLIDAY PARTY |

| sig-05 | TRIPE Event 5 Crosby |
| sig-06 | Cabinet Meeting 01 Feb 2016 |

### 1.5 Audit Period Assignment

The table t_audit_for_period embodies axiom 5 by linking each audit process X to its audit period Y. Column id is the primary key. Columns x and y are entity references; join on x to t_audit_conducted_by retrieves the conducting person for any given period.

| id | x | y |
|----|---|---|
| per-01 | Court Convened Meeting 15 Dec 2015 | 15 Dec 2015 16:00 |
| per-02 | Ministerial Diary Feb 2016 | 1 Feb 2016–29 Feb 2016 |
| per-03 | AUTUMN HOLIDAY PARTY | October 2003 |
| per-04 | ESTROGEN ZONE Session | 2:00 pm 07 Oct 2003 |
| per-05 | TRIPE Event 5 Crosby | October 2003 |
| per-06 | Cabinet Meeting 01 Feb 2016 | 1 Feb 2016 09:00 |
