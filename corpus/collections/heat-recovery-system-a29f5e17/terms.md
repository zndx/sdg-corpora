# Grounding terms

The ontology universals this collection's chapters are written around (each an sdg: class, grounded in a BFO/CCO genus).

## Condensing Heat Recovery System
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A manufactured industrial system designed to capture waste thermal energy from exhaust streams and transfer it to process fluids.
- **attributes**: systemIdentifier, systemModel, designCapacity, commissioningDate, deliveryMode, status
- **relations**: installedAt→PulpMill, capturesFrom→ExhaustStream, deliversTo→HeatSink

## Pulp Manufacturing Facility
- **genus**: `bfo:0000004`
- **definition**: A physical industrial plant where wood pulp is processed, characterized by specific infrastructure and operational zones.
- **attributes**: millIdentifier, location, country, operationalStatus, primaryProduct
- **relations**: hosts→HeatRecoverySystem, hosts→SteamTurbine, hosts→AirHeatingUnit

## Pulp Dryer Exhaust
- **genus**: `bfo:0000002`
- **definition**: A gaseous effluent stream generated by the pulp drying process, characterized by specific thermal and humidity properties.
- **attributes**: streamIdentifier, sourceProcess, temperature, humidityLevel, flowRate, foulingPotential
- **relations**: capturedBy→HeatRecoverySystem, originatesFrom→PulpMill

## Thermal Energy Recipient
- **genus**: `bfo:0000004`
- **definition**: A physical system, fluid, or space that receives thermal energy transferred from the heat recovery system.
- **attributes**: sinkIdentifier, sinkType, targetTemperature, flowVolume, unitCount
- **relations**: suppliedBy→HeatRecoverySystem, locatedIn→PulpMill

## Electricity Generating Steam Turbine
- **genus**: `bfo:0000004`
- **definition**: A mechanical device that converts thermal energy from steam into rotational mechanical energy for electricity generation.
- **attributes**: turbineIdentifier, powerOutput, steamInputRate, operationalSeason
- **relations**: fedBy→HeatSink, locatedIn→PulpMill

## Industrial Process Water
- **genus**: `bfo:0000002`
- **definition**: Water used in industrial processes such as boiler feed or bleach plant makeup, characterized by volume and temperature.
- **attributes**: waterIdentifier, usageType, flowRate, temperature, preheatedBy
- **relations**: heatedBy→HeatRecoverySystem, consumedBy→PulpMill
