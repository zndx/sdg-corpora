# Relational Modeling of Descriptive Information Content Entities in Audit Processes

This chapter specifies four tables that instantiate the provided OWL axioms for descriptive information content entities, supporting traces, and audit processes. The tables use primary and foreign keys to encode subclass and existential restrictions, enabling verification of claims about outlier attributes within audited datasets.
