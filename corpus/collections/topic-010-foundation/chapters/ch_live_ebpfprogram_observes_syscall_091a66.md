COMPLIANCE REGISTRIES FOR eBPF PROGRAM GOVERNANCE RELY UPON A FORMALIZED RELATIONAL MODEL IN WHICH CHECKSUM, IDENTIFIER, ROLE, SUBJECT, AND TARGET CONSTITUTE THE FUNDAMENTAL DIMENSIONS OF TRACEABILITY AND ACCOUNTABILITY. CHECKSUMS PROVIDE THE MECHANISM BY WHICH PROGRAM BINARIES ARE VERIFIED AGAINST UNAUTHORIZED MODIFICATION; IN THE ATTACHMENT REGISTRY, THE PROGRAM lsm_bpf_audit CARRIES THE INTEGRITY HASH 7b14de08, WHILE cgroup_sock_bind AND kprobe_tcp_connect SHARE THE IDENTICAL CHECKSUM 9d2b7a16, SUGGESTING EITHER A COMMON BUILD ARTIFACT OR A SHARED SOURCE MODULE. SUCH COLLISIONS DEMAND CAREFUL AUDITING, AS IDENTICAL CHECKSUMS MAY MASK DISTINCT DEPLOYMENT CONTEXTS OR, CONVERSELY, INDICATE LEGITIMATE REPLICATION ACROSS ENCLAVES. THE CHECKSUM FIELD THEREFORE SERVES NOT MERELY AS A VERIFICATION TOKEN BUT AS A GOVERNANCE SIGNAL—WHEN TWO PROGRAMS ATTACHING TO DIFFERENT HOOKS (lsm VERSUS tc_ingress) SHARE A HASH, THE ONUS FALLS ON THE OPERATOR TO CONFIRM THAT THE DUPLICATION IS INTENTIONAL AND DOCUMENTED.

IDENTIFIERS ANCHOR EVERY ENTITY IN THE REGISTRY TO A STABLE, HUMAN-READABLE REFERENCE THAT SURVIVES REBUILD CYCLES AND ENVIRONMENTAL MIGRATIONS. THE SYSCALL-OBSERVATION DOMAIN EMPLOYS THE PREFIX SYSC—SYSC-0001 THROUGH SYSC-0004—TO LABEL eBPF PROGRAMS SUCH AS tracepoint_block_rq_issue, cgroup_sock_addr, track_memory, AND monitor_network, WHILE THE KPROBE ATTACHMENT TABLE USES THE KPRO PREFIX (KPRO-0001 THROUGH KPRO-0004) FOR PROGRAMS LIKE lsm_bpf_audit AND perf_software_counter. BEYOND THESE INTERNAL KEYS, EXTERNAL IDENTIFIERS APPEAR IN THE FORM OF ARN:res/41 AND DOI REFERENCES (doi:10.1109/x), LINKING LOCAL REGISTRY ENTRIES TO CLOUD RESOURCE ARN AND ACADEMIC PUBLICATION DOIs RESPECTIVELY. THIS MULTI-SCHEME IDENTIFICATION STRATEGY ENSURES THAT AN eBPF PROGRAM CAN BE TRACED ACROSS DOMAINS—FROM INTERNAL DEPLOYMENT TO EXTERNAL CREDENTIALING—WITHOUT LOSING ITS PROVENANCE.

THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN SUBJECT AND TARGET FORMS THE SPINE OF THE GOVERNANCE MODEL, WITH EVERY INTER-TABLE LINK EXPRESSED AS A DIRECTED PAIR: THE SUBJECT IS THE ENTITY INITIATING OR BEARING THE RELATIONSHIP, WHILE THE TARGET IS THE ENTITY RECEIVING OR BEING REFERENCED. IN THE SYSCALL-OBSERVATION JOIN TABLE, THE SUBJECT COLUMN (ebpfprogram_id) AND TARGET COLUMN (observes_syscall_id) TOGETHER WITH THE ROLE COLUMN DEFINE EXACTLY HOW ONE PROGRAM RELATES TO A SYSCALL. FOR INSTANCE, THE RECORD WITH ID SYSC-0002 POSITS EBPFPROGRAM SYSC-0002 AS THE SUBJECT AND SYSCALL SYSC-0005 AS THE TARGET UNDER THE ROLE observer, WHEREAS THE RECORD WITH ID SYSC-0004 ASSIGNES THE SAME SUBJECT SYSC-0002 A DIFFERENT TARGET (SYSC-0006) UNDER THE ROLE contributor. THIS SAME PATTERN RECURS IN THE SUBCLASS-TO-HOOK ATTACHMENT TABLE, WHERE EBPF-0001 APPEARS AS BOTH SUBJECT (ATTACHING TO EBPF-0001 ITSELF UNDER ROLE reviewer) AND AS TARGET (BEING REFERENCED BY EBPF-0003 UNDER ROLE observer), DEMONSTRATING THAT SUBJECT AND TARGET ARE NOT INTRINSIC PROPERTIES OF AN ENTITY BUT CONTEXTUAL ROLES ASSIGNED PER RELATIONSHIP.

ROLE SEMANTICS PROVIDE THE NARRATIVE LAYER THAT TRANSFORMS MECHANICAL LINKS INTO MEANINGFUL GOVERNANCE RELATIONSHIPS. THE OBSERVATION DOMAIN EMPLOYS THE ROLES contributor, owner, AND observer—A PROGRAM THAT CONTRIBUTES TO A SYSCALL MAY BE SHARING DATA OR EVENT CONTEXT, WHILE AN OWNER EXERCISES DIRECT CONTROL, AND AN OBSERVER MONITORS WITHOUT INTERVENTION. THE SUBCLASS DOMAIN INTRODUCES THE ADDITIONAL ROLE reviewer, SUGGESTING A QUALITY-ASSURANCE OR COMPLIANCE-APPROVAL FUNCTION THAT DOES NOT EXIST IN THE SYSCALL LAYER. THIS DIVERGENCE IN ROLE VOCABULARY ACROSS DOMAINS IS NOT ANOMALOUS; IT REFLECTS THE DISTINCT COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS OF SYSCALL MONITORING VERSUS KERNEL HOOK ATTACHMENT. A REVIEWER ROLE IMPLIES THAT THE SUBCLASS-HOOK RELATIONSHIP HAS UNDERGONE SOME FORM OF VALIDATION, WHEREAS AN OBSERVER IN THE SYSCALL CONTEXT SIMPLY TRACKS ACTIVITY WITHOUT ENFORCING POLICY.

THE INTEROPERABILITY OF THESE FIVE DIMENSIONS—CHECKSUM, IDENTIFIER, ROLE, SUBJECT, AND TARGET—ENABLES A COMPREHENSIVE AUDIT TRAIL. AN AUDITOR CAN BEGIN WITH A CHECKSUM (7b14de08), TRACE IT TO ITS IDENTIFIER (KPRO-0001, PROGRAM lsm_bpf_audit), FOLLOW THE SUBJECT-TARGET RELATIONSHIPS TO DETERMINE WHICH HOOKS IT ATTACHES TO (lsm), AND EXAMINE THE ROLES ASSIGNED (reviewer, observer) TO ASSESS COMPLIANCE. ALTERNATIVELY, ONE CAN START FROM A SYSCALL (openat, CONNECT, STATX, SOCKET), TRACE BACK THROUGH THE OBSERVATION TABLE TO IDENTIFY THE SUBJECT eBPF PROGRAMS (SYSC-0001, SYSC-0002, SYSC-0003, SYSC-0004), AND VERIFY THEIR CHECKSUMS AND IDENTIFIERS ACROSS REGISTRIES. THIS BIDIRECTIONAL TRACEABILITY—FROM PROGRAM TO BEHAVIOR AND FROM BEHAVIOR TO PROGRAM—IS THE ESSENTIAL PROPERTY THAT MAKES THE RELATIONAL MODEL SUITABLE FOR REGULATORY COMPLIANCE, INCIDENT RESPONSE, AND CONTINUOUS MONITORING IN PRODUCTION eBPF DEPLOYMENTS.
