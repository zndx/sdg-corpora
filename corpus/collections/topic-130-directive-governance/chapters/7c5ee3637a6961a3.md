# Traceability of Compliance Claims in Regulatory Data Systems

This chapter specifies five relational tables that instantiate the ontology axioms for claim support, evidence requirements, lineage artifacts, anomaly detection, and attestation events. The tables are grounded in the domain of insurance regulatory filings under 11 NYCRR, enabling verification that each descriptive information content entity meets minimum evidence thresholds through primary-key and foreign-key linkages.

## 1. Table Definitions and Axiom Embodiment

### 1.1 t_trace_supports_claim
Axiom 1 is embodied by this table: each row records an individual of type bfo:0000015 that supports a cco:DescriptiveICE. Column id is the primary key; column x holds the supporting entity identifier.
