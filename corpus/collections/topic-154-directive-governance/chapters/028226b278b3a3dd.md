# Chapter 4: Evidence and Compliance Structures in GDPR Audit Trails

This chapter defines five tables that capture directive and descriptive information content entities required for lawful personal-data processing under GDPR. The tables implement the axioms directly: each row is an instance of a subclass of cco:DirectiveICE or cco:DescriptiveICE, and foreign-key links encode the existential restrictions on lawful basis, evidence requirement, refutation, attestation and trace support.
