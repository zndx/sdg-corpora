# Grounding terms

The ontology universals this collection's chapters are written around (each an sdg: class, grounded in a BFO/CCO genus).

## Distributed Denial of Service Attack
- **genus**: `bfo:0000015`
- **definition**: A malicious attempt to disrupt normal traffic of a targeted network, service, or server by overwhelming the target or its surrounding infrastructure with a flood of Internet traffic.
- **attributes**: attackId, attackType, layer, peakBandwidth, startDateTime, endDateTime, isMitigated
- **relations**: targets→NetworkService, detectedBy→FortiDDoSAppliance, mitigatedBy→VerisignScrubbingCenter

## FortiDDoS On-Premise Mitigation Appliance
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A purpose-built hardware or virtualized appliance deployed on-premise to detect and mitigate DDoS attacks in real-time using sophisticated filtering technologies.
- **attributes**: applianceId, model, firmwareVersion, deploymentLocation, maxThroughputGbps, isAlwaysOn, lastHealthCheck
- **relations**: monitors→NetworkService, sendsAlertTo→VerisignDDoSService, generatesAlertFor→DDoSAttack

## Verisign DDoS Protection Cloud Service
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A cloud-based, scalable DDoS protection service that provides on-demand mitigation, traffic scrubbing, and expert analysis for attacks exceeding on-premise capacity.
- **attributes**: serviceId, platformName, apiEndpoint, supportLevel, isActive, lastUpdated
- **relations**: integratesWith→FortiDDoSAppliance, usesPlatform→AthenaPlatform, providesMitigationFor→DDoSAttack

## Verisign Athena Scrubbing Platform
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: Verisign's globally distributed, purpose-built proprietary platform used to protect against volumetric and complex application layer attacks through traffic redirection and scrubbing.
- **attributes**: platformId, region, capacityTbps, isOperational, lastMaintenance
- **relations**: hostsScrubbingCenter→ScrubbingCenter

## Traffic Scrubbing Center
- **genus**: `bfo:0000015`
- **definition**: A physical or logical facility where malicious traffic is filtered and clean traffic is returned to the customer's network during a DDoS event.
- **attributes**: centerId, location, currentLoadPercent, isAcceptingTraffic, lastTrafficRedirect
- **relations**: processesTrafficFor→DDoSAttack, belongsTo→AthenaPlatform

## Protected Network Service
- **genus**: `bfo:0000015`
- **definition**: An internet-facing infrastructure, application, or service that is the target of DDoS attacks and requires protection.
- **attributes**: serviceId, serviceName, ipAddress, port, protocol, criticalityLevel, isInternetFacing
- **relations**: isTargetOf→DDoSAttack, isMonitoredBy→FortiDDoSAppliance, receivesCleanTrafficFrom→ScrubbingCenter

## DDoS Mitigation Alert
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A signal generated by the on-premise appliance when attack thresholds are exceeded, triggering evaluation and potential traffic redirection by the cloud service.
- **attributes**: alertId, severity, thresholdExceeded, generatedDateTime, isAcknowledged, status
- **relations**: generatedBy→FortiDDoSAppliance, receivedBy→VerisignDDoSService, relatesToAttack→DDoSAttack
