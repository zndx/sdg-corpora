# Data Safeguard Ontologies in Relational Audit Models

This chapter defines five ontological templates that classify directive and descriptive information content entities for regulatory compliance. The templates are realized as five tables whose primary-key and foreign-key columns encode the subclass and existential restrictions stated in the axioms. Cross-table joins recover multi-hop relations such as the baseline rate associated with a given technical safeguard.

## 1. Table Definitions and Axiom Embodiment

## 2. Cross-Join Demonstration

To retrieve the PCI DSS requirement that applies to the same baseline rate used by a technical safeguard, join t_pci_dss_requirement to t_lift_with_baseline_rate on y = id, then join the resulting lift row to t_hipaa_safeguard_technical on y = id.  

Example query path:  
R001.y = L002.id → L002.y = T002.id  

Resulting joined row:  
(R001, CardholderDataProtection, L002, AES256Encryption, T002, UniqueUserID)
