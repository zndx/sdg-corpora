# Directive Governance in Regulatory Compliance Systems

This chapter presents five ontological axioms that constrain directive information content entities and related processes within regulatory frameworks. The axioms are projected into a fixed relational schema whose tables record policies, enforcement responsibilities, verification steps, attestation events, and scope restrictions. All column values are drawn from the insurance-regulation domain referenced in consolidated regulatory amendments.

## 1. Axiom-to-Table Mapping

Each table directly implements one axiom. Primary-key columns uniquely identify axiom instances; columns named x and y hold entity references that participate in the axiom’s slot fillers. Joins are performed by matching values across these reference columns.
