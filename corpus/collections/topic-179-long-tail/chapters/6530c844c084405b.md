# Chapter 4: Formalization of Audit Assertions and Governance Controls

## 4.1 Scope and Preamble

This chapter establishes the formal criteria for defining audit scope, specifically within the context of automated compliance frameworks and governance ontologies. It delineates the structural relationships between data artifacts, temporal audit periods, responsible agents, and control directives. The definitions provided herein are aligned with the upper-level ontology BFO (Basic Formal Ontology) and the Common Core Ontologies (CCO), ensuring interoperability across regulatory reporting systems. Implementers must ensure that all audit assertions are grounded in the artifacts described below, maintaining strict referential integrity between the audit event and the dataset under examination.

---

## 4.2 Definition of Datasets Under Audit

### 4.2.1 Regulatory Context

In accordance with governance frameworks for information management, a distinct entity must be classified as an artifact before it may be subjected to audit procedures. The classification of a dataset determines the applicable retention schedules, access controls, and verification protocols. The following section defines the necessary characteristics of a dataset subject to audit, ensuring that the object of the audit inquiry is formally recognized within the system.

### 4.2.2 Ontological Definition

**DATASET UNDER AUDIT** means an information content entity (ICE) that is a subclass of `cco:Artifact` and is currently subject to a `bfo:0000015` (Process) of audit. This entity represents the static or dynamic data collection (e.g., financial ledgers, access logs, configuration files) from which evidence is derived.

**Axiom 2 (dataset_under_audit):**
*   **Manchester Syntax:** `Class: {X:Class} SubClassOf: cco:Artifact, sdg:underAudit some bfo:0000015`
*   **Verbalization:** {X} is something that under audit process.

## 4.3 Audit Periodicity and Temporal Scope

### 4.3.1 Audit Scope Highlights

Following the organizational restructure of the governance unit in Q1, several audit processes have been formalized to cover specific temporal windows. The mapping of an audit process to a specific dataset period is critical for establishing the "Time of Audit" versus the "Time of Record."

*   Audits must be explicitly linked to the dataset version defined in Section 4.2.
*   The temporal boundary of the audit process (`bfo:0000015`) must encapsulate the creation and modification timestamps of the target data.
*   Several opportunities for optimization in the audit trail have been identified and closed during this period.

### 4.3.2 Ontological Definition

**AUDIT FOR PERIOD** refers to a process (`bfo:0000015`) that is specifically scoped to examine a dataset artifact for a defined duration. This relationship ensures that the audit findings are temporally bound to the state of the artifact at the time of the review.

**Axiom 1 (audit_for_period):**
*   **Manchester Syntax:** `Class: {X:Class} SubClassOf: bfo:0000015, sdg:forAuditPeriod some {Y:Class}`
*   **Verbalization:** {X} is something that for audit period {Y}.

## 4.4 Assignment of Audit Responsibility

### 4.4.1 Governance Manual Entry

**MANUAL LETTER #04-2024**
**GOVERNANCE AND COMPLIGENCE**
**TITLE 410 AUDIT STANDARDS AND RESPONSIBILITIES**

**4-100 DEFINITIONS**

**AUDIT CONDUCTED BY** means a standard characterized by the explicit assignment of a natural person (`cco:Person`) to an audit process (`bfo:0000015`). This assignment creates a chain of custody for the audit findings and ensures that a responsible agent is accountable for the verification of controls. The assignment must be recorded in the system prior to the commencement of the audit execution phase.

### 4.4.2 Ontological Definition

The axiom governing this section establishes the necessity of an agent. An audit process, to be valid within this framework, must be conducted by a person. This excludes purely automated batch jobs from being classified as "Audits" in the strict governance sense unless a person is assigned as the responsible overseer.

**Axiom 3 (audit_conducted_by):**
*   **Manchester Syntax:** `Class: {X:Class} SubClassOf: bfo:0000015, sdg:conductedBy some cco:Person`
*   **Verbalization:** {X} is something that conducted by person.

### 4.5.2 Ontological Definition

**AUDIT SUBCLASS** represents a specialization of the audit process where the specific target is a Control Directive Information Content Entity (`cco:DirectiveICE`). Unlike a general data audit, an audit subclass in this category tests the validity, implementation, and efficacy of a specific governance rule or policy encoded within the system.

**Axiom 4 (audit_subclass):**
*   **Manchester Syntax:** `Class: {X:Class} SubClassOf: bfo:0000015, sdg:auditsControl some cco:DirectiveICE`
*   **Verbalization:** {X} is something that audits control directive information content entity.
