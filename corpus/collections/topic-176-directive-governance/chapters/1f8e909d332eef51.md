# Chapter 7: Directive Governance Processes in Municipal Audit Trails

This chapter defines four process classes drawn from an OWL ontology fragment and shows their projection into a four-table relational schema. The schema supports verification, audit, and attestation activities that arise in the preparation of board memoranda and committee minutes for Riverside Public Utilities and the Westchester County Committee on Labor & Housing.

## 1 Ontology Axioms and Verbalizations

Four axioms govern the classes. Each axiom states that its subject is a bfo:0000015 process; two axioms add cardinality restrictions on evidence production or signer participation. The verbalizations are used verbatim in the column comments of the generated tables.

### 1.1 Relational Projection of the Axioms

The axioms map deterministically to the four tables below. Primary keys are the id columns. Foreign-key relationships are realized by storing identifiers from one table inside the x or y columns of another table, enabling equi-joins that traverse the evidence and signer constraints.

t_verification_min_one_evidence embodies AXIOM 1. Its primary key is id; column y stores the identifier of a required evidence process and therefore functions as a foreign key into any table whose id column enumerates admissible evidence instances. A reader can join this table to t_audit_basic on y = id to retrieve the concrete audit record that satisfies the “min 1 evidence” restriction for a given verification.

## 2 Cross-Join Demonstration

To determine which audit record supplies the mandatory evidence for the March 2023 water-production verification that was later attested, execute the following two-hop join:

```
SELECT v.id, v.y, a.x
FROM t_verification_min_one_evidence v
JOIN t_audit_basic a ON v.y = a.id
WHERE v.x = 'MonthlyWaterProductionVerification'
  AND v.id = 'ver-2023-031';
```

The result row is:

ver-2023-031 | aud-2023-014 | WaterProductionAudit

A second join from aud-2023-014 into t_attestation_min_one_signer on the shared process lineage yields the attestation identifier att-2023-007, confirming that the verification–audit–attestation chain is complete for the March 2023 memorandum.
