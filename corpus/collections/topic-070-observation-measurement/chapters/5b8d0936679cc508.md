# Chapter 4: Modeling Directive Governance and Attestation Structures

This chapter defines five core ontological templates for directive governance processes and demonstrates their projection into a relational schema. The schema supports compliance documentation for GDPR articles and SOC 2 trust criteria through attestations, supporting evidence, and audits. All instances are drawn from regulatory reporting contexts.

## 1 Scope and Schema Overview

The model uses five tables derived directly from the supplied axioms. Primary keys are universally named `id`. Foreign-key columns enforce the subclass and evidence relationships required by the axioms. Four to six rows per table suffice to illustrate multi-table joins while remaining compact.

## 3 Cross-Join Example

To retrieve the supporting evidence for a GDPR article attestation that was signed by a person, join `t_gdpr_article_subclass`, `t_attestation_signed_by`, and `t_attestation_with_supporting_evidence` on the declared foreign keys:

```sql
SELECT g.x AS article, a.x AS attestation, e.x AS evidence
FROM t_gdpr_article_subclass g
JOIN t_attestation_signed_by a ON g.y = a.id
JOIN t_attestation_with_supporting_evidence e ON e.y = g.id
WHERE g.x = 'Article5_1';
```

Result row:  
`article = Article5_1`, `attestation = GDPR_Article_5_Attestation`, `evidence = Evidence_Log_2017`
