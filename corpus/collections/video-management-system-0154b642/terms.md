# Grounding terms

The ontology universals this collection's chapters are written around (each an sdg: class, grounded in a BFO/CCO genus).

## Enterprise Video Management System
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A software system that manages, records, and controls video feeds from surveillance cameras and analytics engines.
- **attributes**: systemIdentifier, versionNumber, deploymentMode, isScalable, lastMaintenanceDate, manufacturerId
- **relations**: integratesWith→VideoAnalyticsModule, manages→SurveillanceCamera, operatesIn→ControlCenter

## Integrated Video Analytics Module
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A software component providing rule-based detection and classification of events within video streams.
- **attributes**: analyticsId, ruleCount, isMultiRuleEnabled, detectionType, sdkVersion, processingLocation
- **relations**: runsOn→VideoManagementSystem, analyzes→VideoStream, generates→ForensicEvidence

## Surveillance Camera Unit
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A physical imaging device capturing video feeds for security and transportation monitoring.
- **attributes**: cameraSerialNumber, cameraType, resolution, isEmbeddedAnalytics, installationDate, firmwareVersion
- **relations**: feeds→VideoStream, locatedAt→SurveillanceZone, managedBy→VideoManagementSystem

## Video Data Stream
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A continuous digital data flow containing visual information from a camera or analytics source.
- **attributes**: streamId, bitrate, frameRate, codecType, encryptionStatus, recordingStatus
- **relations**: originatesFrom→SurveillanceCamera, processedBy→VideoAnalyticsModule, displayedOn→VideoWall

## Multi-System Control Center
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A centralized facility housing operators, displays, and management software for surveillance operations.
- **attributes**: centerId, centerName, hasVideoWall, has2_5DMaps, operatorCapacity, operationalStatus
- **relations**: hosts→VideoManagementSystem, displays→VideoWall, monitors→SurveillanceZone

## Forensic Evidence Record
- **genus**: `cco:ont00000995`
- **definition**: A recorded data artifact generated by analytics to support investigation and legal proceedings.
- **attributes**: evidenceId, eventTimestamp, evidenceType, confidenceScore, isVerified, storageLocation
- **relations**: generatedBy→VideoAnalyticsModule, relatedTo→SurveillanceZone, storedIn→VideoManagementSystem
