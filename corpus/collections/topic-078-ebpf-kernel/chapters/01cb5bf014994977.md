# Directive Governance in Regulatory Compliance Ontologies

This chapter presents five relational tables that instantiate axioms for directive information content entities under the cco and sdg namespaces. The tables encode lawful basis, jurisdiction, claim support, evidence requirements, and scoped equivalence relations. Primary and foreign key constraints enforce the slot restrictions stated in each axiom, permitting verification of multi-hop relations through standard equi-joins.

## 1 Schema Overview

The five tables correspond directly to the supplied axioms. Column domains are restricted to entity references (VARCHAR identifiers standing for Class individuals) or literals as required by the slot types. Foreign-key declarations implement the existential restrictions and equivalence conditions without introducing additional columns.

## 2 Cross-Table Query Example

To determine the jurisdiction and evidence requirement supporting a given lawful basis, join t_gdpr_lawful_basis to t_policy_with_jurisdiction on y = id, then to t_control_with_evidence_requirement on y = id. For lawful basis LB-001 the join yields:

- x = ConsentProcessing
- jurisdiction x = EU-GDPR-Article6
- evidence requirement x = Article32Security

The resulting row confirms that ConsentProcessing operates under EU-GDPR-Article6 jurisdiction and requires Article32Security evidence.
